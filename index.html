Your four exercises

01

Push-ups

Chest · shoulders · triceps

Keep your body straight; lower under control. Use a comfortable range and stop before form breaks.

02

Prone pulls

Upper back · rear shoulders

Lie face down, reach arms forward, then draw elbows toward your ribs and squeeze shoulder blades. This is a low-resistance option, not a full replacement for rows.

03

Bodyweight squats

Thighs · glutes

Sit hips down and back, keep feet planted, then stand smoothly.

04

Glute bridges

Glutes · hamstrings

Lie on your back, knees bent; lift hips by squeezing glutes, pause, then lower slowly.

Warm up for 5 minutes. Rest 60–120 seconds between sets. Leave about 1–2 good reps in reserve.
